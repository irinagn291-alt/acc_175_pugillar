Pugillar is a shared daily journal for two people who use one iPhone or iPad. You write your note. They write theirs. Neither of you can read the other side until both notes are in and you save the day.

• Today's page with a rotating question — tap Next for another prompt
• Two named writers on one device. Pass the phone. The other note stays hidden until you save
• Save today's page when both people have written. Midnight will not auto-save a half-written day
• This week’s strip on home — which days you already saved
• Word count and write streak on today's page
• Together: days since you started, streak, this month, marks at 7 · 30 · 90 · 365
• Past days: month calendar, search, favorites, open a day, share that day
• Daily reminder in Settings so you do not skip a page
• Edit both names, export the whole book, reset all data
• Everything stays on this device. No account, no purchases, no cloud

Use defaults names the two people Alex and Sam and starts today.
