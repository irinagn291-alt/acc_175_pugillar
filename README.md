# Pugillar

Ink both plates. Seal the seam.

Pugillar is a pair diary for two people who share one device. Home is a diptych: two shuttered wax plates on one bronze hinge. Each hand writes unseen. Seal opens both halves, files the leaf, and freezes it. A one-sided night does not archive — that leaf stays home after midnight. Bond counts sealed leaves and days since the bond date, never word counts. There is no account, no feed, and no Game tab.

## Architecture

Blind-seam encoding. A single `LeafStore` owns today's `Leaf`, both `Plate`s, the `Bond`, and the `Shelf`. Views bind a `DiptychSnapshot`; they never read a shuttered plate and never mutate a sealed leaf. `SeamSeal` stays dead until both plates hold non-blank ink. A legal seal writes the `PairEntry`, freezes that file, and drops a blank leaf as the new home. If a plate is still missing after midnight, that same leaf remains the home root.

This pattern fits the product: the verb is seal-the-seam, not save-a-journal. A tab plus a list of notes would be a clone.

SwiftUI hosts one `PugillaresHinge` UIView. Two CALayer wax recesses take ink under shutter layers; a `CATransform3D` hinge opens only after Seal. Bond, Shelf, and Settings arrive as full-screen overlays. Today's diptych never leaves.

## Blind-seam seal

This is why a pair would pick Pugillar. Neither half is readable until both have written. Typing is allowed; reading the foreign plate is not — hit-testing and VoiceOver refuse it, so unreadability is not colour alone. Seal stays dead until both plates have ink. Sealing opens the hinge, writes the pair entry, and freezes the leaf. Two-halves prompts unlock B only after A answers. Bond days use `startOfDay(now) − startOfDay(bondedAt)` with marks at 7, 30, 90, and 365.

## Design

Wax pugillares: boxwood boards, black wax, bronze hinge, stylus, lampblack smear. Palette lives in `Assets.xcassets` and is reached only through `WaxFace.Palette`: background `#100C08`, surface `#261E14`, ink `#EDE3CF`, accent `#C08A3E`, muted `#8A7B62`. Type is Hoefler Text in a six-step scale; shuttered captions use HoeflerText-Italic; the seal verb uses HoeflerText-Black; bond figures use old-style numbers. At the largest accessibility size, chrome falls back to New York. Spacing unit 8 pt. Tap targets 44 pt. Hard wax edges.

## How this is not a repeat

`pair_diary` is unused in the 41-app ledger. Home is two shuttered plates, not a shared notes list, a couple chat, a year-tone canvas, or a mood pet. The home verb is seal-the-seam. A one-sided day refuses to archive. No public feed. No Game tab.
